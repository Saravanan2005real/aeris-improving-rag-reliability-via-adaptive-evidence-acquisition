## 1. Executive Summary

The independent audit of Step 22D identified a critical architectural defect in the retrieval-memory integration:
> *In `app/adaptive_retrieval/controller.py:172-178`, when `memory.has_query_attempt(...)` detected a cache hit, the loop executed `continue` without populating `all_evidence` or `all_chunk_ids`. Consequently, downstream claim extraction and answer generation were starved of evidence, forcing an immediate false downgrade to `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.*

Under **Step 22D.1**, this defect has been completely resolved through four focused improvements:
1. **Evidence Payload Preservation & Delivery:** `QueryMemory` and `EvidenceMemory` schemas now record serialized `EvidenceItem` structures. On a valid cache hit, `AdaptiveRetrievalController` deserializes the cached items, appends them to `all_evidence`, updates coverage metrics identically to live retrieval, and delivers the full evidence set to downstream stages.
2. **Safe Fallback on Incomplete / Malformed Entries:** If a cached entry lacks reconstructible evidence or fails parsing, it is treated as a cache miss, increments `fallback_count`, and safely executes live hybrid retrieval rather than returning empty evidence.
3. **Thread Safety & Bounded LRU Capacity:** `RetrievalMemoryRegistry` was upgraded with reentrant locking (`threading.RLock()`) and an `OrderedDict`-based bounded LRU cache (`max_capacity=1000`) to guarantee thread safety and prevent memory leaks.
4. **End-to-End Preflight Gate & Regression Suite:** Gate Check 4 in `scripts/step22d_preflight_gate.py` and a new test suite (`tests/test_step22d1_retrieval_memory_repair.py`) now execute end-to-end assertions verifying non-empty evidence delivery, identical chunk IDs between cached and live runs, depth incompatibility, document/session isolation, and failure under the historical defect.

## 5. Telemetry Validation

The controller and memory telemetry now cleanly distinguish and record:
- `memory_lookup_count`: Total query attempts evaluated against memory.
- `memory_hit_count`: Total compatible query attempts where valid cached evidence was returned.
- `memory_miss_count`: Total query attempts where no compatible entry existed.
- `memory_skip_count`: Total queries skipped due to scope/document/session divergence.
- `memory_cached_evidence_count`: Total individual `EvidenceItem` objects delivered from memory.
- `memory_fallback_count`: Total fallback executions triggered by missing, corrupt, or unparseable cached evidence.
- `memory_incompatibility_count`: Total candidate queries rejected due to candidate depth or requirement mismatch.

A cache hit is **never** reported as successful evidence delivery unless non-empty, valid evidence items were reconstructed and appended to `all_evidence`.
